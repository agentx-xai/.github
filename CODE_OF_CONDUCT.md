# Code of Conduct

AgentX aims to be a welcoming, constructive open-source project. Participants must be respectful, assume good intent, give and receive actionable feedback, and avoid harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or publishing private information.

Project maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation when behavior violates this standard. Report a concern privately through the contact method in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) or to the maintainers of the affected repository.
